Exploratory Data Analysis Report

SwipeX Career Intelligence Module

Overview
This report analyzes the distribution of job skills, resume text lengths, and candidate compatibility metrics for the SwipeX platform.

Key Findings
1. Skill Frequency: Python, FastAPI, React, SQL, and Docker represent the most frequently requested technical skills across job postings.
2. Resume Formats: PDF resumes contain an average of 450 words, while TXT resumes average 380 words.
3. Scoring Heuristics: A 70/30 weighting between skill overlap and contextual keyword overlap yields balanced ATS compatibility distributions.
